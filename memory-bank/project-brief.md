# Product and Business Context

## Company

Nexova Solutions is a human resources consulting and talent acquisition firm founded in 2011. It operates in Valencia, Spain, with a second office in Miami, Florida, and serves mid-sized companies that need help hiring, training, and supporting talent at scale.

## Business model

Nexova works across three main business lines:

- executive and mid-management headhunting
- customer support outsourcing for technology, retail, and finance clients
- corporate training in soft skills and leadership

The company is not a consumer product business. It is a B2B services company operating in people operations, support services, and talent management.

## Who uses the back office

The back office is used by internal operational teams that need to manage service delivery, people workflows, and customer-facing operations from a single place.

The main users are:

- support supervisors monitoring queue health, backlog, agent workload, and SLA performance
- team leads assigning incidents to the correct area and following up on resolution progress
- operations managers reviewing service quality across clients and channels
- account managers and client-facing teams tracking the health of customer support contracts
- internal Nexova teams responsible for dispatching work, maintaining service status, and escalating critical cases

This back office is not a generic admin dashboard. It is the operational control layer for the outsourced support business.

## What an incident means in this operation

In this context, an incident is any customer issue raised by a client company on behalf of its end users or customers. It can arrive through phone, email, or chat, and it must be managed as a tracked operational case rather than a loose conversation.

For Nexova, an incident represents:

- a customer problem or request that must be handled by an assigned team
- a case with a channel, priority, severity, category, and owner
- a workflow item that has a current status and a responsible area
- a record that must be auditable over time

Operations teams need to record where the incident came from, how it was classified, who owns it, and what state it is in at any given moment.

This is essential because support operations depend on visible queue health, SLA compliance, and correct routing. If an incident is not tracked well, the team loses accountability and the client experience suffers.

## Operational requirement

The system must allow operations teams to:

- register an incident regardless of the source channel
- classify it according to the rules defined by the business context
- assign it to the correct responsible area
- track its current status over time
- know who changed the incident and when the change happened

This last point is non-negotiable: when an incident changes status or owner, the system must preserve an audit trail of who changed it and when. Without that history, the incident management system is not trustworthy.

## Strategic challenge

Nexova has a strong reputation and domain expertise, but it lacks the infrastructure to operate at scale. Many critical processes remain manual, especially in operations and support. The company needs a system that gives visibility, traceability, and faster coordination across teams.

## Business objective

The objective is to give Nexova operational teams a reliable way to manage issues from intake to resolution, with clear ownership, traceability, and visibility over service quality.
