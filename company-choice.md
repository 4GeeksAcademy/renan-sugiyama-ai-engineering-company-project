## My AI Agent Idea

- **What the agent would do:** Receive a job vacancy, extract its key requirements, analyze available candidate CVs, compare each candidate against the role, calculate a compatibility score, rank the candidates, and explain why each candidate received that score.
- **Information it would need:** The job description, required and preferred skills, experience level, languages, location or availability constraints, candidate CVs, and optionally historical recruiter feedback or previous successful placements.
- **What it would produce or trigger:** A ranked shortlist such as Candidate A — 92% match, followed by strengths, missing requirements, and relevant evidence from the CV. Candidates above a configurable threshold could automatically move to the recruiter-review stage, while lower-scoring profiles remain searchable in the candidate database.
